## Quaternius conflict remains OPEN

<https://quaternius.com/license.html> currently states "Quaternius Asset
License (QAL) v1.0", "Last updated: 8/28/2026".

- Section 2 permits incorporating assets into a Product and distributing that
  Product, and authorizing contractors/collaborators to work on it.
- Section 3 prohibits redistribution of original or modified assets as
  standalone assets, packs, stock files, templates, or similar products.
- Section 7: "Changes will not apply retroactively to Assets you've already
  obtained under an earlier version; the version in effect at the time you
  obtained the Assets governs your use of them."

The nature pack's committed `License_Standard.txt` expressly states CC0 1.0.
The asset-specific raft/paddle pages also still state CC0. This disagreement
must not be silently resolved by treating all Quaternius content as either
CC0 or QAL. A release date before the QAL date is not proof of when this team
obtained an asset. Obtain asset-specific clarification before a separate
asset/source distribution. Repository privacy does not settle license rights.

The configured repository was observed to be public during this task. The
owner instructed proceeding with its existing configuration after being
informed; no repository visibility change is authorized or performed. This
does not constitute resolution of the licensing conflict.
